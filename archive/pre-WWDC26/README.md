# Pre-WWDC26 Roadmap Archive

Archived on July 16, 2026.

This directory preserves the assumptions and schedule from the May 19, 2026
roadmap. It is historical context only and is not part of the active learning
plan.

## Why it was retired

- The calendar targeted January 2027 and assumed roughly 32 study hours each
  week.
- It organized learning around isolated projects and infrastructure tools
  rather than competency gates.
- It hard-coded Gemini 2.5, ADK 1.x-era concepts, Vertex Agent Engine, and
  pre-WWDC26 Apple APIs.
- System design began too late and did not continuously cover AI, iOS, and
  backend systems.
- RAG, agents, infrastructure, and Apple AI were treated as separate topic
  blocks instead of parts of coherent production systems.

## Original phase map

1. May 19–25, 2026: setup and account creation.
2. May 26–July 27, 2026: Python, FastAPI, Doc-Talk, Docker, and webhooks.
3. July 28–September 28, 2026: OSS-Docs RAG, Cloud Run, Redis, and SLOs.
4. September 29–November 30, 2026: researcher agent, GKE, Terraform, and
   Apple Intelligence.
5. December 1, 2026–January 17, 2027: system design, interviews, and the FDE
   playbook.
